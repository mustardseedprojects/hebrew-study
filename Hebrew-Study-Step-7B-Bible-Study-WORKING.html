Follow the Threads of Scripture

Some studies begin with a Hebrew word. Others begin with a question, a repeated theme, or a connection between passages. This is where we can follow those threads more deeply through Scripture.

Our Approach to Scripture

This portion of the site is intentionally Christian. We approach the Bible as the Word of God and study it from that perspective. Our studies look at Biblical Hebrew, historical and cultural context, and connections throughout Scripture, allowing Scripture to help interpret Scripture. Our purpose is not only to understand what the text says, but to grow in our understanding of God, His Word, His Covenant, and His Kingdom.
