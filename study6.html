Genesis 1:6–7

❧

6And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters.

7And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so.

If I were to place value on the concepts of mathematics, I would approach it like this:

Adding or multiplying = good.

Subtracting or dividing = bad.

Adding always seems easier, and normally, when things compound, I’m happy with the outcome… mostly. Of course, I can think of things that I hate when they compound, like health issues, weight, or interest I owe a bank.

But in my recent Bible study, it occurred to me how God used division.

As a matter of fact, in Genesis 1, God divided several things and gave them a new identity.

He divided the light from the darkness, creating day and night. He created the firmament by separating the waters above from the waters below.

This led me to wonder what else God separates in order to create something new.

Leviticus 20:24 says, “I am the LORD your God, which have separated you from other people.”

Deuteronomy 10:8 says, “At that time the LORD separated the tribe of Levi, to bear the ark of the covenant of the LORD, to stand before the LORD to minister unto him, and to bless in his name.”

And this was a new identity for the Levites, but also a new identity for us when we accept Jesus as our Lord and Savior.

The same Creator from Genesis 1 sets us aside as His own when we commit to Him.

1 Peter 2:9 says:

“But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light:”

I love the creation language that is used here! We have been separated out of darkness and into His marvelous light!

In Acts 13:2, it talks about Barnabas and Saul:

“…the Holy Ghost said, Separate me Barnabas and Saul for the work whereunto I have called them.”

What a privilege to be chosen and sent!

Let’s talk about the power behind God’s division.

In Genesis 1:9–10, God gathered the waters together, causing dry land to appear. A boundary was established.

Proverbs 8:29 says:

“When he gave to the sea his decree, that the waters should not pass his commandment…”

The power in this restraint provides a way for life to be sustained. Were it not for the boundaries God established, the dry land would remain covered by water.

I am so thankful for the sacrifice God made that set in place a boundary for us, separating us and ensuring our place in eternity.

Acts 4:12 says:

“Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.”

This division makes us His children.

God’s been doing this since Genesis… and it still holds.

❧

Life Application

We often welcome addition and multiplication, but resist subtraction and division. Yet Scripture shows God using separation to establish order, identity, and purpose.

Is there a boundary or season of separation in my life that I have assumed must be bad?

Not every separation is God's direction, and not every loss is good. But we can ask Him for wisdom to recognize when a boundary protects us or a change makes room for a new calling.

How can I respond with faith, patience, and obedience while I seek His purpose?
